Research internship with **Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services (Speech)**, Summer 2021.

- Built a **personalized keyword spotting** system fusing RNN-T keyword detection with
  **Res2Net speaker verification**, cutting false-accept rate **16%** while staying
  resilient to noisy and adversarial input.
- Benchmarked CNN and RNN-T models against **FGSM/PGD** attacks on Google Speech Commands
  and Cortana datasets, quantifying robustness gaps that informed production model selection.

Mentors: Jian Wu, Yong Zhao.
