Two granted US patents from my work on robust speech recognition:

- **[US 11,335,329](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11335329)** — _Method and System
  for Generating Synthetic Multi-Conditioned Data Sets for Robust ASR._ Filed Mar 2020,
  granted 2022.
- **[US 11,322,156](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11322156)** — _Features Search and
  Selection Techniques for Speaker and Speech Recognition._ Filed Dec 2019, granted 2022.

This synthetic-data and feature-robustness work predates — and anticipates — much of
today's interest in data augmentation for reliable speech models.
