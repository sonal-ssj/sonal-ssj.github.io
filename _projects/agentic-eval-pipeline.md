As part of **ARPA-H's CARE program**, I lead the technical execution of a
safety-evaluation suite for patient-facing medical chatbots — coordinating 10+
clinicians, engineers, and graduate students.

At its core is an **agentic evaluation pipeline** (LangChain, Langfuse) that triages
AI-flagged false positives and false negatives *before* they reach a clinician. The goal:
meaningfully reduce expert annotation burden while preserving clinical grounding — so
that scarce clinician time is spent only where automated judgment is genuinely uncertain.
